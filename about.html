Women’s Venture Fund

The organization behind Keymakers.

The Women’s Venture Fund helps women establish thriving businesses in urban communities through lending, advisory services, and business development programs. Keymakers is WVF’s storytelling movement—connecting entrepreneurs through the keys that opened their doors.

Our Work

How WVF opens doors.

Founded in 1994, WVF is a nonprofit and Certified Community Development Financial Institution (CDFI) focused on practical access for women entrepreneurs.

01

Lending

Small loans and capital access that help women-led businesses launch, stabilize, and grow.

02

Advisory

Entrepreneurial training, technical assistance, and advisory services tailored to real business challenges.

03

Partnerships

Building capital networks and program partnerships that extend opportunity for women in urban communities.
