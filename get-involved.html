Founding Members help build what’s next.

Founding Members

Keymakers who join through an eligible paid membership during the founding period will be recognized as Founding Members—helping shape the movement from the start.

Members of the Host Committee may also be recognized as Founding Members according to approved program rules. Founding Member status is not automatic for event attendance, free membership, Connector service, or Key Guide roles.

Programming

Doors to Capital

Doors to Capital brings together WVF business development programs, preparation for financing conversations, peer learning, and relationship-building—so Keymakers can grow ready for loans, investment, and other pathways when the moment is right.

Doors to Capital represents programming and opportunity. Participation does not guarantee funding, investment, a loan, an introduction, or approval.
