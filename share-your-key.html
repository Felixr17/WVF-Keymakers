Share Your Key

What was the key that changed what was possible for your business?

It may have been a person who opened a door, access to capital, an important relationship, a difficult decision, a lesson learned the hard way, or a resource you finally found.

Women’s Venture Fund would like to record a brief conversation with you about your key and the story behind it. Your experience will become part of the Keymakers library—a growing collection of stories, knowledge, resources, and connections designed to help more women entrepreneurs find their way forward.

What to expect

  • The conversation will take approximately 15–20 minutes.
  • You do not need to prepare a speech or produce your own video. A member of the WVF team will guide the conversation and record your answers.

During the conversation, we’ll ask about:

  • What your key was
  • What challenge or closed door you were facing
  • How that key changed what was possible
  • What you learned that could help another woman
  • What door you would now like to help open for someone else

Tell us you are interested

Complete the short form below, and a member of the WVF team will contact you to arrange your conversation.

A few words are enough. We will explore the full story during the conversation.
