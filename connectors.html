What Connectors Do

Turn relationships into opportunity. Connectors bring people together, open doors, and help more women belong, grow, and lead.

Bring People

Create meaningful connections across the community.

Make Introductions

Help the right people find each other at the right time.

Strengthen the Movement

Expand opportunity and create a more inclusive, connected future.

Connector is a volunteer role, not a membership tier. Connectors build relationships; they do not guarantee introductions, capital, contracts, or other outcomes.

Key Guides

Experienced Keymakers and/or Connectors may apply to become Key Guides, offering practical guidance, insight, and support inside the members-only Community Circle. Key Guides help others navigate, grow, and make the most of their journey—separate from the relationship-building focus of Connectors.
