Why Keymakers exists now

Somewhere in every entrepreneur’s journey, someone handed her a key.

Sometimes it was an introduction. Sometimes a mentor, a customer, an investor — or simply one person who believed in her before anyone else did. Whatever it was, someone opened a door.

Keymakers exists to name those moments out loud — and to make sure the next woman finds her key a little faster than the last one did.
