Privacy

This page describes how the public Keymakers website uses information you choose to send us. It is not a substitute for a legal review by Women’s Venture Fund counsel.

What we collect on this website

  • Share Your Key: name, email, phone, optional organization, your key story, participation preference, optional membership questions, and contact permission.
  • Newsletter and Gathering updates: email address.
  • Connector and Key Guide interest: name, email, optional phone and organization, whether you are already a Keymaker, a short message, and contact permission.
  • Membership checkout is handled by Givebutter on their site, not by these pages.

How it is used

WVF uses submissions to follow up about stories, events, volunteer roles, and membership. Submitting a story is not permission to publish your name, photo, or likeness. Paid Community Circle access is confirmed through membership systems that are separate from this public website.

Contact

Questions about privacy or a request related to your information: info@wvf-ny.org or (212) 563-0499.
