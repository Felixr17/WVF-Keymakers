Terms of use

These terms apply to the public Keymakers website operated for the Women’s Venture Fund. They are a website-facing summary, not a complete legal agreement.

Using this site

You may browse stories, community information, and ways to get involved. Do not submit information about someone else without their permission. Do not use the forms to send unlawful, harassing, or misleading content.

No guaranteed outcomes

Sharing a key, joining a list, applying as a Connector or Key Guide, attending programming, or becoming a member does not guarantee publication, funding, introductions, contracts, Community Circle access on a specific date, or any other outcome. Connector and Key Guide are volunteer roles, not membership tiers.

Membership

Paid membership is processed by Givebutter. Community Circle access begins at Keyholder membership and is scheduled through WVF’s member systems, not as an instant login on these public pages. Key Carrier (free) does not include Community Circle access.
