Accessibility

Keymakers should be usable with keyboard, screen reader, and on small screens. Pages include skip links, labels on form fields, visible focus on primary controls, and respect for reduced-motion preferences.

Known limits

  • Some photography is atmospheric stock until WVF supplies official images; alt text is written so stock faces are not named as members.
  • Membership checkout and video playback use third-party services (Givebutter, YouTube) with their own accessibility support.
  • The members-only Community Circle, when it opens, will live off this public site.

Report a barrier

Email info@wvf-ny.org with the page URL and what you were trying to do. You can also call (212) 563-0499.
